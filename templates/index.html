### Step 4: Add the Whitelist to Railway

Your new script looks for an environment variable named `WHITELIST`. You need to add this in your Railway dashboard.

1.  Go to your project on Railway and click into your service.
2.  Go to the **Variables** tab.
3.  Click **+ New Variable**.
4.  **Variable Name:** `WHITELIST`
5.  **Value:** A comma-separated list of Instagram usernames you want to protect. **Do not put spaces after the commas.**
    *   *Example:* `nasa,elonmusk,a_friend_of_mine,another_user`
